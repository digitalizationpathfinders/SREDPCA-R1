Before you begin

Use this web form to provide information about your planned Scientific Research & Experimental Development (SR&ED) project. You must fill out a separate form for each of your projects.

  • Anticipated project start and end dates
  • Main problem or unknown of your project, and why it is an issue
  • Type of work you will do to resolve the problem or unknown
  • Types of data or results you will collect or analyze
  • Total SR&ED expenditures
  • Project planning documents that outline the timeline, key milestones, and major activities or phases. For example:
    • Project plans
    • Gantt charts
    • Work breakdown structures
    • Schedules
    • Planning briefs
  • Documents describing planned internal controls for tracking SR&ED work and associated expenditures, including documentation practices, and how you will maintain simultaneous documentation throughout the project. For example:
    • Time-tracking processes
    • Documentation protocols
    • Internal review procedures
    • Controls for capturing experimental records.
  • Key supporting references and documents that demonstrate evidence of due diligence in determining that existing knowledge, solutions, approaches cannot address the scientific or technological uncertainty and your hypothesis, proposed solution or approach. For example:
    • Scientific articles and technical papers
    • Meeting minutes
    • Design specifications
    • System architecture diagrams
    • Photographs
  • List of project team members, including potential or prospective project members and their respective areas of expertise and credentials.

Describe the problem or uncertainty

Fields marked with asterisk ( *) are required.
What scientific or technological uncertainty are you aiming to address? Include the objective of the project and describe the new scientific or technological knowledge, or the new or improved capability you are seeking (500 words) To answer this question you must include answers to following questions:
  1. What goals will you be trying to achieve in this project?
  2. What new or improved knowledge, process, or capability you are hoping to develop?
  3. What technical challenge or limitation prevents this from being straightforward?
  4. Describe the issue that can not easily be solved using known methods or existing technology.
  5. Explain the gap in current knowledge, or the potential constraint that you may not be able to be overcome.
  6. Describe what would be the really cool thing that you figured out that no one else has or knows.
