Scientific Research & Experimental Development (SR&ED)

Pre-claim approval (PCA) overview

Submitting a PCA can help you:

  • get approval for future SR&ED projects (for up to 3 years)
  • receive relevant information directly from CRA
  • carry out an accelerated 90-day expenditure only review (on approved projects)

Each PCA can contain a maximum of 3 projects. If a PCA is not submitted or modified after 18 months, it will be deleted.
